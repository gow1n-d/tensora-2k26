Why TENSORA?

More Than a Conventional Hackathon

TENSORA 2026 brings together student innovators, developers, designers, and AI researchers to solve high-stakes challenges with working prototypes.

AI Is Not Optional

Every solution must demonstrate meaningful AI/ML implementation (deep learning, computer vision, NLP, or LLM agents). Decorative or static algorithms receive zero AI score.

Problems With Purpose

Challenges curated across Transport, Healthcare, Agriculture, Education, and Sustainable Energy rather than abstract synthetic coding puzzles.

Build Online → Defend Offline

An intensive 24-hour remote development sprint followed by an elite offline grand finale where shortlisted teams present live prototypes before industry leaders.

National Stage Exposure

Compete alongside top student engineering teams across India, gain direct industry mentorship, and earn recognized cash prizes, trophies, and certificates.

Regulations

Official Hackathon Rulebook

Ensure your team complies with all guidelines governing participation, code originality, and IP ownership.

Rule 01: Eligibility & Teams

Open to enrolled UG/PG students across recognized Indian colleges. Teams must comprise 2–4 members. Cross-college teams are fully permitted.

Rule 02: Build Window

All core coding and pipeline construction must take place between 21 Sept 6:00 PM and 22 Sept 6:00 PM IST. Pre-built apps are strictly prohibited.

Rule 03: 100% IP Ownership

Participants retain complete ownership of the intellectual property they independently create during the hackathon.

Rule 04: Open Source & APIs

Public open-source libraries, frameworks, and foundational model weights are allowed with proper citation in the README.

Rule 05: Submission Deliverables

Teams must submit public GitHub repo, setup documentation, 3-min video demo link, and presentation slide deck before the deadline.

Rule 06: Zero Plagiarism

Direct copying of other teams' code or submitting existing commercial products will result in immediate disqualification.
